Questions, ideas or something not working? Here are quick answers, and a way to reach us.

How does Worthit? work out the time?

It turns your monthly take-home pay into an hourly rate using the hours a day and days a week you work, then divides the price by that rate. One month of work always equals one month of your pay.

How do I change my pay or hours?

Open the Settings tab. Changes apply straight away.

I bought Pro. How do I get it on a new phone?

Pro is tied to your Apple Account. Open Settings → Customise your desk and tap Restore. With Family Sharing on, your family can use it too.

I have a code for Pro

Open Settings → Customise your desk and tap Redeem code.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings → Delete all data, or delete the app. See our Privacy Policy.
