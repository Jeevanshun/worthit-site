Worthit?

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Worthit? is built so your money stays your business. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your take-home pay, currency, working hours and days, age and retirement age, and your recent checks are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. The Worthit? widget and the share extension read them from your phone, on your phone.

What syncs through your iCloud

If you use Worthit? Pro, the way you've customised your desk (window, drink, outfit and so on) is synced between your own devices using Apple's iCloud. It contains no money or personal details, and we can't see it.

Feedback you choose to send

If you use Ideas & feedback in the app, or the form on our Support page, your message, and your email address only if you choose to add one, are sent to the developer through Formspree, a form delivery service. We use them only to read and reply to your feedback, and never for marketing. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Purchases

Worthit? Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether Pro is unlocked.

Sharing

Share cards are made on your phone. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it, and then only where you send it. The price is left off the card unless you switch it on.

Children

Parents may use Worthit? with their children, for example with pocket money. We don't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete all data in the app, or delete the app. To have feedback you sent deleted, contact us through the Support page.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to us through the Support page.
